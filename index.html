Link Cleaner — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 22, 2026

This policy describes how the Link Cleaner mobile app ("Link Cleaner", "the app") handles information. It is short on purpose, because the app collects almost nothing.

The short version: Link Cleaner does not collect, share, or sell any personal data. It works entirely on your device. The only network access is to expand short redirect links to their final destination. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

What the app does

Link Cleaner removes tracking parameters (such as ?igsh=…) from links you share or paste, and copies the cleaned link to your clipboard. It can also follow short links (for example bit.ly) to find the real destination.

Data we collect

None. Link Cleaner does not ask you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers.

What is stored on your device

The app stores the following locally, on your phone only:

  • Your recent cleaning history (so you can revisit links you cleaned).
  • Your settings and whether you have seen the onboarding screen.

This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling Link Cleaner deletes all of it. You can also clear your history from within the app.

Network access

The app requests the INTERNET permission for one reason: when a link you clean is a short/redirect link, the app contacts the destination server to discover where the link finally leads. The link (and only the link) is sent to the website it points to, exactly as any browser would. No other data is transmitted.

Third parties

Link Cleaner does not include advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or tracking SDKs. We do not share data with any third party because we do not collect any.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to access, export, or delete on our end. Everything the app keeps lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall it.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date revised.
